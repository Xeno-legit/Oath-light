# Oath Light
Master plan

 Oath light is an anti-addiction software that is designed specifically for fighting Pornography and the fast spreading virus of such disgusting and shameful acts regardless. The app should go under 4-7 stages of development.

The First 3 stages are beta stages, then the rest is Alpha stages

Until every stage is complete or before it by one stage.

## What makes it different

▶ Every other blocker is good at one thing and bad at the rest. Oath light has to be good at all five.

* Nothing NSFW gets through, on any surface, in any app.
* On-device AI that catches what lists can't — and acts on it.
* Support in the weak moment, not just a wall.
* The weak-moment self can't outvote the strong-moment self.
* Free, open, private. The only blocker you never have to trust blindly.

## Rules we don't break

▶ These were decided already. Changing one needs a reason, not a mood.

* No false positives. A blocked normal site is worse than a missed porn site.
* Turning protection on is instant. Turning it off waits.
* Serious mode is all or nothing — no per-feature exceptions to argue with.
* The app shows status, never a map of itself.
* Nothing leaves the device unless the user set it up themselves.
* The AI can cover the screen and nothing else. No irreversible actions.
* Solo users get the full product. A trusted contact is a bonus, never required.
* Noir is the only built-in theme. Everything else is user colours.

NOTES

- Domains marked as "borderline" are close to being dedicated adult sites
  but still have some non-adult usage
- Many of these platforms have legitimate uses - blocking them entirely
  may cause false positives
- Consider implementing path-level blocking instead of domain-level
  (e.g., block reddit.com/r/nsfw* instead of all of reddit.com)
- Some domains may already exist in the main blocklist with subdomains
- Shorteners cannot be effectively blocked without collateral damage
